<objective>
Make LVGL v8 render on the CYD (ESP32-2432S028R) panel and fix the black-screen root cause (D-35). Today this env builds pure TFT_eSPI direct-draw with `HANSOLOminerv2` and `lvgl` ignored; there is no LVGL bridge. This plan adds LVGL to the env, supplies an env-local `lv_conf.h`, writes the LVGL-over-TFT_eSPI display bridge (flush callback + draw buffer + driver registration) modeled on the in-fork `wt32DisplayDriver.cpp` pattern, and ramps the backlight on boot so pixels appear within seconds. It also resolves the Clock price up/down colors (D-36) by disassembling the recovered handler, replacing the tentative `0xf01d`.

Purpose: every screen module in waves 2-3 depends on a working LVGL surface; without the render bridge the device stays black (the user's reported fault).
Output: a compilable LVGL surface for the CYD env, a brightness ramp, and finalized price colors in the palette.
</objective>
